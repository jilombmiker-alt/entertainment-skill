# Design patterns and originality boundary

Use comparable products only to understand mechanics. Never reproduce their proprietary card wording, artwork, layouts, brand voice, decks, or paid content.

| Source | Transferable principle | This skill's adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Storymatic — https://thestorymatic.com/pages/the-two-laws-of-the-storymatic | Modular character and situation combinations | Authored light prompts with bounded story change |
| Story Engine — https://storyenginedeck.com/products/the-story-engine-deck-of-prompt-cards | Random modules remain under creator control | Deterministic draw plus user-controlled pass and format switch |
| Dixit — https://www.libellud.com/en/our-games/dixit-odyssey/ | Ambiguous imagery supports multiple readings | No “correct” interpretation and no personality claims |
| We're Not Really Strangers — https://www.werenotreallystrangers.com/pages/how-to-play-core-game | Gradual interaction and a closing beat | Optional light reflection with no minimum card count |
| {THE AND} — https://shop.theskindeep.com/pages/couples-edition-skuv2 | Explicit right to pass | Pass, lighter, change topic, or stop at every step |
| The School of Life Teamwork Game — https://www.theschooloflife.com/shop/teamwork-game/ | Categorized prompts plus pass card | Safe starter families with exclusions and non-disclosure mode |

The distinctive promise is: reproducible randomness + story-first facilitation + reflection only by consent. Preserve all three.
