# Design patterns and originality boundary

Use these products only as mechanism references. Do not copy their text, routes, puzzles, artwork, characters, brand voice, or paid content.

| Source | Transferable principle | This skill's adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Story City — https://about.storycity.app/inside-the-frightnight-story-every-choice-counts/ | Identity, branching choices, stateful endings | Bounded AI dialogue around verified public places |
| Geocaching Adventure Lab — https://www.geocaching.com/sites/adventure-lab/ | Location-based checkpoints and flexible sequence | Optional evidence methods with privacy-preserving fallback |
| Questo — https://questoapp.com/ | Landmark observation plus story progression | Shorter adaptive route rather than a fixed purchased quest |
| VoiceMap — https://voicemap.me/ | Low-screen, place-triggered narrative | Compact story beats and one checkpoint at a time |
| Goosechase missions — https://support.goosechase.com/en/articles/4437526-creating-your-missions | Photo, text, and location task types | Player-selected verification with equal-quality endings |

The distinctive product promise is: verified city facts + a fictional parallel identity + state that changes the next checkpoint. Preserve all three; otherwise the output collapses into a tour, a scavenger list, or pure role-play.
